Start the companion

TrueDub translates and voices videos with a small app on your computer. Start it in a terminal (Terminal on macOS, PowerShell on Windows):

cd truedub/companion
uv run truedub

Keep it running while you watch. It listens on 127.0.0.1:7861.

Press Shift + D on a video to toggle the dub.
